## W6-005 — vendor-race defect: CDN presence outranked a fired wall

economist.com's 403 carries Cloudflare presence headers
(cf-ray, server: cloudflare) and DataDome's fired wall
(x-datadome header + captcha-delivery body). Detection order
made Cloudflare the vendor (first block wins) and the verdict
WALLED — wrong on both counts: the wall that answered is
DataDome, and a fired challenge is CHALLENGED.

**Generalized fix (not a per-site rule):** wall-signal precedence
in `_detect_vendor` — when several vendors are detected, the first
vendor with a *wall* signal (challenge/block marker) wins over
vendors present only as CDN/sensor presence; with no wall signals,
the first detected vendor stands. This generalizes the
PerimeterX-over-CDN principle (Fiverr) and the Kasada-over-Akamai
principle (W5-003) into one rule, and makes the classify() special
cases redundant (kept, harmless). Regression fixture S4e pins the
economist shape; the 41-capture corpus diff stayed clean (only
the npmjs flapping case, explainable).
